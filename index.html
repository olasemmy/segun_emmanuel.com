Sustainability

Why it shapes how I work

I started in architecture. My BTech thesis looked at how daylighting can improve energy efficiency and comfort in a modern ICT centre in Ibadan. Managing real projects then showed me the gap between strong technical delivery and the strategic, cross-functional management needed to deliver sustainably.

That is the gap I’m closing. Through IESE (Strategy and Sustainability), Lund University (Sustainable Cities and Communities) and now my Master’s at IAE Dijon, I’m learning to put ESG at the centre of strategy. My thesis explores sustainability strategy in French SMEs.

I want to bring this to brand, project and ESG roles in France and across Europe.
